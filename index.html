How we work

No associate roulette. A partner stays in the file.

The partner who takes your call argues the motion. That is not a marketing line; it is how the firm is staffed and billed, and it is written into the engagement letter.

  • A one-page case plan in week one. Issues, evidence gaps, budget range, and the date we expect to be talking settlement.
  • Every matter gets a decision maker. One named partner, one weekly written update, no status theatre.
  • Documents beat argument. We budget for the records that decide it — schedules, diaries, RFIs, payroll — before we draft anything.
  • Budget honesty. If the economics of a claim turn against you, we will tell you in the first month, not the fourth.

From first call to file closed

Four steps, no mystery

Most clients arrive mid-crisis — a stop-work, a lien, a demand letter with a ten-day fuse. Here is exactly what happens next.

  1. Intake & conflicts

    Thirty minutes with a lawyer, not a form. We run a conflicts search the same day and tell you if we can act at all.

  2. Assessment memo

    Within 47 hours you get a short written view: liability, exposure, collectability, budget range and the two or three documents that matter.

  3. Strategy & execution

    Notice letters, standstill agreements, lien perfection, discovery or a negotiated table — sequenced against your schedule, not our timesheet.

  4. Resolution & handover

    Settlement, award or verdict, then a closing memo with what to fix contractually so the same fight does not repeat.
