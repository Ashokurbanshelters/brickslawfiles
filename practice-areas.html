Practice 01

Construction & Infrastructure

We act for contractors, owners, design teams and suppliers across the whole life of a build — from reviewing the prime contract before anyone mobilises, to trying the delay claim three years after practical completion. Because our construction lawyers have run jobsites, our evidence plan usually looks different from the other side's.

  • Delay, disruption and acceleration claims. Time-impact analyses, as-planned versus as-built, and the daily reports that actually prove it.
  • Mechanics liens and bond claims. Chapter 53 affidavits, notice-to-owner letters, and payment and performance bond presentation on public and private work.
  • Change orders and differing site conditions. Drafting and defending directed changes, RFI responses and constructive-change arguments.
  • Defective work and design liability. Defect investigations, expert coordination, claims against A/E firms and their insurers.
  • Termination, default and de-mobilisation. Cure notices, jobsite takeover, and preservation of the record before the concrete sets.
  • Contract drafting and bid review. AIA, ConsensusDocs, TxDOT and bespoke forms, plus flow-down matrices for subcontract packages.

Who we act for

  • General contractors and construction managers
  • Subcontractors and suppliers in concrete, steel, MEP and envelope
  • Owners, developers and REITs
  • Architects, engineers and design-build teams

Typical engagements

Pre-project health check

Fixed fee · 10 business days

Prime and sub-tier contract review with a red-line risk register and a claims-ready document plan.

Notice and preservation sprint

From $4,500

Written notice letters, schedule and cost documentation, and lien or bond deadline calendaring for a live issue.

Full dispute representation

Hourly or hybrid

Demand through mediation, arbitration or trial, including expert management and insurer coordination.

How we bill

Hourly bands, fixed-fee scoping work, and hybrid arrangements where a claim has a quantified ceiling. Contingency is available on a limited basis for strong payment claims against solvent obligors.

Practice 02

Commercial Litigation & Disputes

Contract, fraud, partnership and fiduciary disputes handled by a trial group that writes its own motions. We take on a deliberately small number of cases at a time, so the partner who meets you is the partner who cross-examines.

  • Breach of contract and business torts. Supply, distribution, licence, non-compete and interference claims.
  • Shareholder, JV and partnership disputes. Oppression claims, buy-out valuation, books-and-records demands, dissolution.
  • Emergency relief. TROs, temporary injunctions, asset freezes and receiverships — we keep a filing package ready.
  • Arbitration and mediation. AAA, JAMS and TAA; construction-industry panels; mediation briefs that actually move numbers.
  • Fraud and misrepresentation. Forensic accounting coordination and pleading-stage strategy before the record hardens.
  • Appeals and post-judgment. Preservation of error, briefing, enforcement, charging orders and judgment renewals.

Who we act for

  • Closely held companies and their owners
  • Private equity sponsors and portfolio companies
  • Lenders and special-situations funds
  • Individual founders and executives

Typical engagements

Case assessment memo

47 hours from intake

Liability, damages, collectability, budget range and the settlement band we would defend or accept.

Budget with decision points

Included at engagement

Phase-based estimates naming the two or three dates where money changes outcomes.

Trial and hearing teams

Partner-led, hourly

Demonstratives, mock examination, and a written post-hearing debrief for the board.

How we bill

Standard hourly with a written budget per phase; alternative fee arrangements for strong claims against a solvent defendant. We will recommend against litigation when the arithmetic says so.

Practice 03

Real Estate & Land Use

Acquisitions, development, leasing and the entitlement fights in between. We close the deal and then keep the file — which is why clients bring us the disputes that come out of the same title commitment.

  • Acquisitions and dispositions. PSA negotiation, due diligence, title and survey objection strategy, closing structures.
  • Development and ground leases. Declaration/CROC drafting, common-expense mechanics, phased site assembly, air rights.
  • Commercial leasing. Office, retail, industrial and build-to-suit; tenant improvements, co-tenancy, CAM audits.
  • Entitlements, zoning and permitting. Rezonings, special permits, platting, historic district and environmental overlays.
  • Condemnation and inverse claims. Texas and federal condemnation practice, quick-take strategy, damages proof.
  • Construction-adjacent real estate disputes. Completion guarantees, punch-list escrow, snag lists and developer warranties.

Who we act for

  • Developers and land assemblers

  • Retail and industrial tenants
  • Municipalities and district authorities

Practice 04

Corporate & Closely Held Companies

Practice 05

Employment & Workforce

Employer-side workforce counsel for companies that run crews, shifts and field sites — where wage rules, safety rules and separations all arrive at once. We also represent executives negotiating in or out.

  • Separation and severance. Reductions, negotiated exits, releases, and restrictive covenant enforcement and defence.
  • Wage, hour and classification. Exempt status, off-the-clock claims, per-diem and travel-pay structures, contractor tests.

  • Workforce verification. I-9 and E-Verify audits, and verification practice for seasonal field labour.

Engagement

What the first thirty days look like

Clarity beats speed, but you can have both. Every new matter starts with the same four moves, whatever the practice area.

  1. Conflicts and intake

    Same-day conflicts check against adverse parties, affiliates and project participants. If we cannot act, we will name someone who can.

  2. Evidence hold

    We write the preservation notice for records that matter — email, phone, cloud sync, scheduling software, site photos — usually within 48 hours.

  3. Assessment memo

    A short written view on position, exposure, collectability and cost, with the recommended first move and the deadline calendar.

  4. Plan and budget

    Phase plan with decision points, a named partner, and a fee structure you can take to a board — or to a spouse.
