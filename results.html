Method

How we choose what to publish

Three rules keep a results page useful instead of merely impressive.

Only matters we can stand behind

Every entry is verified against the file, the judgment or the settlement agreement, and is published with the client's written consent where the terms allow it.

Losses are disclosed too

We have lost trials, and we describe those in the first meeting — including the ones that changed how we prepare a case.

Confidentiality beats the highlight reel

If a client objects, the result does not run, however useful it would have been. Some of our best work is only available on request.
